# VerseVision

## v0.1 product promise

**Turn music and creative intent into a time-coded, generator-ready visual blueprint.**

VerseVision accepts a user's music and creative direction, analyzes the track's timing and structure, and returns an organized visual production plan that can be used in an external image or video generator.

## v0.1 boundary

The first release produces planning and prompt artifacts. It does not claim to render a finished music video.

## Expected output

- song timing and beat observations;
- time-coded scenes and shot suggestions;
- generator-ready visual prompts;
- camera, lighting, movement, and editing direction;
- continuity references for characters, locations, props, and visual motifs;
- aspect-ratio adaptations when requested;
- confidence flags when audio analysis is uncertain.

## Lyric-aware planning

Supplied lyrics produce line- and word-level timing cues, explicitly marked provisional when estimated from meter and text rather than acoustically aligned. Those lyric moments are carried into scene prompts so visual direction can respond to actual phrases, not only section labels.

## Product principle

VerseVision should make the next creative tool more effective: the user receives a blueprint that is specific enough to paste into a generator, inspect, revise, and reuse.
